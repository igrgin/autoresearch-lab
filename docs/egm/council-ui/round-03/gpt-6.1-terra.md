**Functionality: 8/10.** The creation form explains the folder name and destination, and saved projects have clear search, sort, and Open controls. The reopened state identifies the current project and confirms success. A few interactions and labels could be clearer.

**Appearance: 8.5/10.** The interface is calm, consistent, and easy to scan, with a clear hierarchy and well-spaced controls. Some secondary text is small and low contrast, and the creation panel feels text-heavy.

Observed issues and improvements, in order of importance:

1. **The opened project’s row replaces its Open button with “Opened.”** This communicates status, but makes it less clear whether the row itself is interactive. Keep a consistent action area and make the current state distinct from an action.
2. **The reopened view offers “Show files in Finder” as its main action.** Since users are reopening a project in a workspace, clarify what they can do in the app now and what opening Finder does. The future-update note helps, but sits low in the panel.
3. **Folder-name guidance is dense.** Shorten the persistent instructions and show specific validation feedback beside the field when needed.
4. **Secondary text is small and muted.** Increase its size or contrast, especially for paths, field guidance, and status messages.
5. **The workspace path is long and visually prominent for routine use.** Place it behind a compact location control or allow it to truncate while retaining the copy action.
