**Functionality: 8.5/10.** Creation requires only a name, and search, sorting, path inspection, and prominent Open controls support finding and reopening projects. The selected row and detail panel clearly agree on the current project. Some location controls and row details need clearer hierarchy.

**Appearance: 8.5/10.** The interface is calm, consistent, and readable, with a clear two-panel layout and well-defined primary actions. The saved-project rows feel crowded, and small secondary text and controls compete for attention.

Observed issues and improvements, in order of importance:

1. **Saved-project rows are crowded.** Folder identifiers, path links, timestamps, and Open buttons sit close together, making rows harder to scan. Give metadata a dedicated second line with more spacing, and align the action consistently at the right.
2. **Location information is split across several controls.** “Where projects are saved,” the parent-folder field, and full-path actions make users piece together how the locations relate. Show the complete destination together in the creation panel, with editing and copying available nearby.
3. **Recency is overly precise for the list.** Second-level timestamps consume space and add visual noise. Use a concise relative label in the row, with the precise timestamp available on hover or in details.
4. **Secondary labels are small and low-contrast.** Parent-folder labels, timestamps, and navigation status text could be easier to read. Increase their size or contrast slightly while keeping them subordinate to project names.
