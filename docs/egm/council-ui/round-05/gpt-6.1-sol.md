- **Functionality: 9/10.** Creation has one clear required field, a resolved destination and a prominent action. Search, sorting, individual Open buttons and persistent navigation make saved projects easy to find and reopen. The reopened state clearly identifies the current project and offers Finder access.

- **Appearance: 9/10.** Calm, consistent and well organized. Typography, spacing and restrained green accents establish a clear hierarchy. The current-project treatment and focused detail panel feel polished.

Specific issues, ordered by importance:

1. **Long paths are difficult to scan.** Both panels wrap paths through folder names. Emphasize the final project folder separately, abbreviate the parent path and keep the complete path available through expansion and copying.
2. **“Show details” remains actionable while those details are already displayed.** The highlighted project and matching panel communicate selection well, but the button adds ambiguity about what another click accomplishes. Give it an explicit expanded state or change its label to “Details shown.”
3. **Secondary text actions receive relatively little visual emphasis.** “Customize,” “Copy path” and “Show full path” are smaller and less clearly button-like than the main controls. Slightly larger targets and consistent hover/focus treatments would improve discoverability and precision.
